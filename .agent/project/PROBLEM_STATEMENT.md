CareBank

Description:
Modern banks provide limited personalized financial 
guidance to customers, often relying on static rules and 
manual monitoring. This mini-project presents a 
Personalized Banking and Financial Wellness System 
using Generative AI and Agentic AI to help customers 
manage their finances more effectively. The system 
continuously analyzes customer transaction data to 
understand spending patterns and financial behavior. 

Details:
Generative AI is used to interact with customers in natural language, generate 
monthly spending summaries, explain expenses, and provide easy-to-understand 
savings tips. Agentic AI is implemented through multiple autonomous agents that 
monitor spending, plan budgets, send alerts for overspending or low balance, and 
recommend suitable banking products such as savings plans or deposits. A 
coordinator agent manages the interaction between these agents and ensures 
timely and relevant actions.
The proposed system works proactively without constant user input, improving 
customer experience, encouraging better financial habits, and demonstrating the 
practical use of Generative and Agentic AI in the banking domain.
